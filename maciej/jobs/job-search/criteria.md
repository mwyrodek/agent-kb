# Criteria

## Roles
- QA Architect, Test Architect, QA Lead, or similar senior QA (e.g. Head of QA, QA Manager, Principal/Staff QA/SDET, Test Lead).

## Location
- Remote anywhere in the EU: OK.
- Hybrid in Poland: OK. Wrocław preferred (rank higher).
- On-site only: exclude unless in Wrocław and otherwise great (flag it).

## Pay
- Target: more than 165 PLN/h net (B2B) or the equivalent.
- Conversions (B2B net): 165 PLN/h ≈ 1 320 PLN/day ≈ 27 700 PLN/month (168 h).
- Currency: fixed rates 1 EUR = 4.2 PLN, 1 USD = 3.6 PLN (set by Maciej; deliberately below market). Target ≈ 39 EUR/h ≈ 46 USD/h.
- "Clearly below" = top of the range under target. Top of the range at or above target → strong.
- UoP monthly gross is not comparable 1:1; keep it and note "UoP" instead of judging pay.
- Offers with no pay listed: keep, do not penalise.
- Offers clearly below target: drop, or list with a "low pay" flag if otherwise strong.

## Contract
- B2B or UoP: both OK.

## Must-haves
- None.

## Avoid if possible (rank lower, flag, do not drop)
- Gambling / betting / casino companies.
- Python-heavy roles (Python is not Maciej's strength).

## Freshness
- Older than 60 days: ignore (do not list).
- No posting date: list, but flag "date?" and check the company careers page if it looks strong.
- Prefer offers from the last 30 days.

## Hard rule
- Never apply. See AGENTS.md.
